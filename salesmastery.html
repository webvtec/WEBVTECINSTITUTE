🚀 YOUR STEP-BY-STEP GUIDE TO GETTING YOUR FIRST CLIENT
1
FIND YOUR CLIENTS — WHERE TO LOOK

Don't wait for clients to come to you. Go where they already are. Here are the best places to find businesses that need WEBV TEC — including spots most reps never think to check.

🗺️ GOOGLE MAPS

Search any business type + your area (e.g. "barber Kingston"). Any listing with no website button is a hot lead. That missing link is your opening.

📸 INSTAGRAM & FACEBOOK

Search local hashtags like #876business, #kingstonfood, #jamaicasalon. No website in the bio = a direct lead. DM them — they're used to doing business online.

🚶 WALK YOUR AREA

Walk plazas, markets, and high streets. Look for signs with no website. Any shop without a URL on their sign or window is a prospect. Walk in with confidence.

📰 FACEBOOK MARKETPLACE & CLASSIFIEDS

People selling on Marketplace or Craigslist Jamaica are running businesses with no proper online presence. Contact them directly — they're already trying to reach customers.

🏘️ COMMUNITY FACEBOOK GROUPS

Join groups like "Kingston Buy & Sell" or "Portmore Community". Vendors, service providers, and event promoters post here daily — and they need exactly what WEBV TEC offers.

🎪 EVENTS, MARKETS & FAIRS

Craft fairs, food markets, church fairs, school events — vendors here almost never have websites. You can close multiple clients in a single afternoon.

📱 WHATSAPP BUSINESS ADS

Watch statuses and business flyers being forwarded in your WhatsApp groups. Anyone promoting a service via WhatsApp with no website is a direct prospect.

👥 YOUR OWN NETWORK

Friends, family, church members, old coworkers — ask everyone: "Do you know any business owner without a website?" Referrals convert fastest because trust is already there.

🎨 SELLING PROMOTIONAL MATERIALS — PITCH, OBJECTIONS & CLOSE
1
WHERE TO FIND LEADS FOR PROMOTIONAL DESIGNS

This is one of the easiest sells you'll ever make — because the proof of the problem is right in front of you. Every bad flyer, handwritten sign, or blurry poster is a commission waiting to happen. Here's how to spot leads and close them.

🪟 SHOP WINDOWS & WALLS
Walk any plaza or high street and look for printed or hand-drawn flyers taped to windows. That's a business that needs design work — walk in and offer to upgrade it.
🚌 BUS STOPS & POLES
Flyers stapled to poles or pasted at bus stops are almost always low-quality. Find the business number, call them, and offer a professional redesign from $20 USD.
📱 WHATSAPP & INSTAGRAM
When you see a blurry or basic promotional image being shared in WhatsApp groups or posted on Instagram — DM that business. They're actively trying to promote; they just need better materials.
🎪 EVENTS & MARKETS
Vendors at fairs, food markets, and pop-ups often have hand-drawn or no signage at all. Walk up, compliment their product, then offer to help their marketing look as good as what they're selling.
⛪ CHURCHES & COMMUNITY BOARDS
Churches, community centres, and schools regularly hold events and almost always use basic flyers. Approach the organiser — ticket design + flyer is an easy $50 conversation.
🆕 NEW BUSINESSES
A brand new shop opening is the perfect opportunity — they need a logo, business cards, and an opening flyer all at once. Stack those three and you're looking at a nice commission.
2
PITCH PROMOTIONAL MATERIALS — SCRIPTS THAT WORK

Promo pitches are faster and more visual. The evidence is right in front of you — a bad flyer, a blurry post, no branding. Point to the problem, offer the fix, keep it short. These close quicker than websites.

IN PERSON — SAW THEIR FLYER OR SIGN
"Hey — I noticed your flyer in the window. I'm a rep for WEBV TEC, a design company. We do professional flyer and poster designs from $20 USD — something a lot sharper, fully designed and delivered to you digitally. Want to upgrade it?"
DM — SAW A BLURRY OR BASIC POST
"Hey [Business]! I saw your recent post — love what you're promoting. I'm a rep for WEBV TEC, a design company. We create professional flyers, logos and event graphics from $20 USD. A cleaner design gets way more attention. Want me to connect you with our designer?"
FOR AN UPCOMING EVENT (tickets + flyer)
"I heard you have an event coming up — are you sorted for tickets and promotional flyers? WEBV TEC designs both from $20 USD each. A professional ticket and flyer makes the event look credible and is way easier to promote and share."
FOR A NEW BUSINESS (logo + cards + flyer)
"Congrats on the new business! Have you sorted your logo and business cards yet? WEBV TEC does logos, business cards, and opening flyers — all from $20 USD each. Getting the branding right from the start makes a big difference."
WHEN THEY ASK "HOW MUCH?"
"Each design starts from $20 USD — flyer, logo, ticket, business card. If you need more than one, the owner can sometimes bundle them at a discount."
3
PROMO OBJECTIONS — STAY CALM & RESPOND
❌ "I already have a logo / flyer."
→ "That's great — do you have a high-quality version you can use for print and social? WEBV TEC delivers a clean, print-ready PNG and JPG so you always have the right file. It's $20 USD and makes a real difference in how professional it looks."
❌ "I make my own flyers on Canva."
→ "Canva is handy — but a custom professional design stands out from the templates everyone else uses. For $20 USD you get something unique to your brand that actually stops people scrolling."
❌ "I don't have the budget right now."
→ "Completely understand. At $20 USD it's one of the most affordable upgrades you can make. If timing's the issue, let me still take your details — the designer can reach out when you're ready."
❌ "Let me think about it."
→ "Of course — let me just take your details and the designer will reach out. No commitment at all, just a quick chat to see what works for you."
4
CLOSE & STACK THE PROMO DEAL

Promo deals close faster than websites — lower price, immediate value, less convincing needed. Get the yes, stack if you can, collect their info, and refer them.

GET THE FIRST YES
"Great — let me connect you with the designer. Can I grab your name, email, and number?"
STACK AFTER THE FIRST YES
"While we're at it — do you have a logo? We can sort that for the same price. The owner might even bundle them at a slight discount if you grab a few at once."
CONFIRM AND WRAP UP
"Perfect — I've noted everything. The WEBV TEC designer will be in touch to confirm the details and get started. Quick turnaround."
